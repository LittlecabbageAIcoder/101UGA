About Us · 關於我們

No. 101 Squadron — the first squadron of HKACC.

To strive, to seek, to find, and not to yield. Formerly No. 1 Squadron, renamed No. 101 Squadron on 9 January 1995. Hong Kong Island Wing · Operations Group. Based at HKUGA College since 2014.

Unit

Heritage
First squadron of the Corps (ex–No. 1 Sqn). Owl badge for wisdom and sharp observation. Commandant's Award runners-up in 2018–19 (two years running).

About HKACC
Founded in 1971 — a registered charitable uniformed youth group under the Security Bureau / Government Flying Service family, offering discipline training, aviation education, exchange, and community service.

Cadets' Life · 團員生活

Why join us?

Saturday discipline and leadership training at HKUGA College — aviation education, foot drill, PT, expedition, first aid, camps, competitions, and exchange.

  • Regular meetings — every Saturday at HKUGA College
  • Aviation education — flying experience, GFS GAINS, AAEP, gliding
  • Discipline & skills — foot drill, PT, expedition, first aid
  • Other activities — joint sqn days, parade, LTC, camps, hiking, service
  • Competitions — Albatross, Commandant's Award, PMCO
  • Exchange — IACE, Mainland & local exchanging opportunities

Journey of Air Cadets · 團員歷程

Join from 11–18. Cadet life continues until age 21.

Membership ends on the first day of April in the year a cadet turns 21. Progress at your own pace — a standard reference path:

  1. Years 1–2 Recruit Cadet · Basic Cadet · Cadet Lance Corporal
  2. Years 3–4 First Class Cadet · Cadet Corporal
  3. Years 5–6 Leading Cadet · Cadet Sergeant
  4. Years 7–8 Senior Cadet · Cadet Flight Sergeant
  5. Years 9–10 Staff Cadet · Cadet Warrant Officer
